Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

youtube-agent ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of a single YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner to manage their own channel. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's YouTube account through Google OAuth with these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos, set their thumbnails and schedule their publication.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's channel and list of uploaded videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owner's channel analytics (for example, views per day) to choose publishing times.

How the information is used

The data is used only to perform the actions above on the owner's own channel. The app does not access other users' accounts or data.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not run a server and does not send YouTube data to any third party.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/trustablebeta/trustablebeta.github.io.
